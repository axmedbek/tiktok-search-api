# Lessons — Signer / argus (v46)

Format per `agent-docs-standards.md`: `- **<Symptom>.** <Cause>. **Fix:** <the standing invariant, phrased as something that IS true>`.

Everything here was measured on TikTok **46.9.x** against the live `api32-core-alisg.tiktokv.com` gateway on 2026-09-10/11, with a decrypt round-trip and three independent hash/MAC matches proving the decryption (see § how it was proven). Do not re-derive it; extend it.

## The big one — argus failures on v46 are PAYLOAD FIELDS, not a dead key

- **A signed request to a user-scoped endpoint returns HTTP 200 with a zero-length body and `tt_orcas_res: 1`, and the reflex is "the sign key is stale / the signer is dead."** The vendored `SIGN_KEY` and its ENTIRE crypto chain (AES-CBC framing, SM3 key schedule, SIMON-128/256, `encrypt_enc_pb`, `mix()`, `dyn_encode`) are **live and correct for 46.9.x** — a real app `x-argus` was reproduced bit-exact from them. What our shipped signer got wrong was the **protobuf payload it encrypts**: ~7 wrong hardcoded field constants, two fields emitted that the app omits (`f5` device_id, `f16` device_token), a stale MSSDK version pair, and three fields it never emitted at all. **Fix:** a 0-byte `tt_orcas_res: 1` on a signed path is triaged as an argus *content* problem (which field is wrong/missing), never as a dead key and never as `hit_shark`. The corrected payload lives in `helpers/argus.py::_corrected_protobuf`, gated on both a captured pair AND the path being in `client.USER_SCOPED_PATHS`; the legacy payload is byte-identical without a pair, which is what keeps `/search` (a different gateway that accepts the legacy payload) untouched.

- **The corrected v46 field set** (measured, each `value << 1` because every scalar in this payload is stored shifted): `f15 = {1: 9<<1, 6: 3<<1, 7: ts<<1}` — `f15.7` is the request `ts`, NOT `app_launch_time`; `f23 = {2: 9<<1, 4: 76593152<<1, 5: 13631488<<1}`; `f25 = 3<<1`; `f28 = 5<<1`; `f33 = 2<<1`; `f29 = 2<<1`, `f30 = 6<<1`, `f31 = 477937796<<1` (legacy emitted `f29=516112` and `f30=6` UNSHIFTED — both wrong); NO `f5`, NO `f16`. Header byte 7 is `0x00` (legacy `0x02`). Outer AES pad is length-only random filler (last byte = pad length); the inner protobuf pad stays PKCS7. MSSDK is `v05.03.01-ov-android` / `84082976` and `dyn_version = 3` (that `dyn_encode` branch reproduced the app's `f26.2` exactly, 12/12 bytes).

- **`x-gorgon` and `x-ladon` are NOT server-validated on `/aweme/v1/aweme/post/`, and neither are argus `f34/f35/f36`.** Measured: our OWN (known-wrong) gorgon and ladon were accepted, and B≡C proved `f34/35/36` make no difference. **Fix:** do not spend effort reproducing gorgon/ladon or reversing `f34/35/36` for this endpoint — they are dead ends. `f34/35/36` are per-request but unvalidated; omit them.

## The (f24, f3) pair — the one thing that must be captured

- **The corrected payload is still refused unless it carries a captured `(f24 dyn_seed, f3 rand)` pair; `f24` alone fails and a freely-chosen `f3` fails.** `f24` is a 98-byte server-issued seed; `f3` is the rand, which also keys `dyn_encode`→`f26.2` (a MAC), so the two must travel together. Measured properties: the **seed is session-scoped** (one seed served 29/30 flows across 17 paths and 5 hosts, re-minted every few minutes by the app's choice), the **rand is endpoint-scoped** (a pair harvested from `/aweme/v2/feed/` positively WORKS on the feed endpoint and positively FAILS on `/aweme/v1/aweme/post/` — same seed, same gateway), one accepted pair is **reusable across requests and different `user_id`s** and stayed valid **≥ 20.6 h**, and **timestamps are free**. **Fix:** to sign the posts endpoint you must capture a pair from a real `/aweme/v1/aweme/post/` request the app itself made — a pair from any other endpoint is refused there. `f24`'s provenance is `POST /ms/get_seed` (response encrypted, 176-byte blob) and the rand pool appears to come from `/ms/dyn/task` (66 KB encrypted response); decrypting those two would remove the capture dependency entirely and is the only path to truly device-free signing, but it needs native `libmetasec_ov.so` work.

- **The pair is cookie-class identity material and lives on the identity, together.** `Identity`/`ClientConfig` carry `dyn_seed` + `dyn_rand`; a half pair is a `ValueError` at construction and `IdentityStore.reload` drops such an entry. **Fix:** never log, print, repr or commit the pair; `dyn_pair*.json` is git-ignored; the pair is extracted OFFLINE from a captured `x-argus` by `tiktoksearch/argus_pair.py` (round-trip-proven, `--self-test`), never taken from a response.

## How it was proven (so a future doubt does not re-run it)

- The decrypt chain: base64 → 2 clear seed bytes → AES-CBC(md5(SIGN_KEY[:16]), md5(SIGN_KEY[16:])) → `[9-byte header][region][2 clear seed bytes][pad]`, `region_end = len(buf) - buf[-1] - 2` → invert `encrypt_enc_pb` (it xors bytes 8.. with data[:4] THEN reverses, so the inverse reverses first — re-applying it is NOT the inverse and produces a plausible-looking wrong result) → SIMON-128/256 decrypt keyed by `SM3(SIGN_KEY ‖ seed4 ‖ SIGN_KEY)[:32]` → protobuf. Verified by: round-tripping our own signature byte-exact, and matching the app's `f13`=`sm3(16 zero bytes)[:6]`, `f14`=`sm3(query_string)[:6]`, `f26.2`=`dyn_encode(v3)`. `tiktoksearch/argus_pair.py --self-test` is the standing regression.

## Endpoint / host facts

- **The user-scoped endpoints live on `api32-core-alisg.tiktokv.com`, not `search19-normal-alisg`.** `_host_for` routes `USER_SCOPED_PATHS` there; `/search` stays on `search_host`. **Fix:** `posts_host` must have no trailing slash — `urlsplit('https://h//aweme/...').path` becomes `//aweme/...`, which is not in `USER_SCOPED_PATHS`, so a stray slash routes correctly but signs with the LEGACY payload → silent 0-byte refusal. Hosts are `rstrip('/')`-normalised for this reason.

- **The real profile path is `/tiktok/user/profile/other/v1`**, not `/aweme/v1/user/profile/other/`. And the profile endpoint refuses even the APP'S OWN valid signature with a 0-byte `tt_orcas_res: 1` while the posts endpoint serves it — so profile is a SEPARATE problem (most likely logged-out: no `sessionid`/`x_tt_token`), not a signing one. **Fix:** do not build profile on the logged-out inference; it needs a logged-in identity measured first.

- **`/aweme/v1/aweme/post/` keys on `user_id` alone.** Sending an unvalidated `sec_user_id` (e.g. a 76-char feed-sourced sec_uid; real profile sec_uids are ~132 chars) yields a 0-byte `tt_orcas_res: 1` refusal. **Fix:** `_posts_params` sends `user_id` only.
